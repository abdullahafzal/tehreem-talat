How does adult therapy work for anxiety, depression, and OCD?

Adult therapy uses Cognitive Behaviour Therapy to change the thought-feeling-action loop behind anxiety, depression, and OCD. Sessions last 45 to 60 minutes. You get a working plan after hour one, then skills and exposure work in the weeks that follow.

Adults often arrive with a story they have already told three people. Friends. Family. Sometimes Google. What they have not had is a method. CBT supplies one. You name the trigger, the thought, the body response, and the behaviour that keeps the loop alive. Then you pick one piece to change this week.

What a typical adult course looks like

  1. Session 1: history, current symptoms, goals, and a recommended method.
  2. Sessions 2 to 4: skills for the main loop, plus homework you can actually do on a workday.
  3. Middle phase: exposure for OCD and phobias, or behavioural activation for depression.
  4. Later sessions: relapse plan so you are not dependent on the chair forever.

EMDR is used when trauma or flashbacks sit under the present complaint. REBT is used when perfectionism, guilt, or "I must" rules are the engine. Both are described on the therapy approaches section of the homepage.

Pro Tip

Write three recent examples of the problem before you arrive. Dates help. "I always feel anxious" is a mood. "Tuesday 11am, meeting lobby, heart racing, left early" is something a clinician can work with.

Which therapy approaches are used in these services?

Cognitive Behaviour Therapy is the core method for anxiety, depression, OCD, panic, and stress. EMDR is used for trauma and PTSD. Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy targets rigid beliefs, overthinking, and self-criticism. The first session decides which one fits.

CBT, EMDR, and REBT in plain language

Approach What it targets Common uses in this clinic
CBT Thoughts and behaviours that keep distress going Anxiety, depression, OCD, panic, social anxiety, phobias, anger, burnout
EMDR Stuck traumatic memories and their body charge PTSD, childhood trauma, grief, flashbacks, attachment wounds
REBT Irrational rules you treat as facts Perfectionism, overthinking, guilt, assertiveness, work and exam stress

Mixing methods without a reason creates noise. One primary approach, adjusted as evidence comes in from homework, is cleaner. That is how these therapy services in Lahore are run.

Psychological testing as part of care

Testing evaluates cognitive, emotional, and behavioural functioning. Adults request it when work or study has stalled and no one can say why. Parents request it when school and home tell two different stories. Results feed the therapy plan. They are not a substitute for showing up to the next session.
