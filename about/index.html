How does she approach therapy sessions?

Sessions use Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, individual psychotherapy, psychological testing, and behavioural interventions. Child work adds play-based methods. Each plan is tailored. Standard length is 45 to 60 minutes in a confidential room, online or at Omar Hospital.

Let's look at the method, not the marketing. CBT asks a simple question: what thought, feeling, and action are looping, and which one can you change this week? You will leave with homework. Not a worksheet for the sake of it. A specific experiment.

Cognitive Behaviour Therapy and related methods

CBT is the backbone for anxiety, depression, OCD, panic, social anxiety, burnout, phobias, anger, and low self-esteem. EMDR is available when trauma sits underneath the present problem. REBT is used when rigid beliefs ("I must never fail") are running the show.

Now that you know the labels, here is the honest part. The method is chosen after the first conversation, not before. A grief case is not an OCD case. Treating them with the same script is how people quit therapy.

  1. First session: history, current difficulties, and goals. No pressure to overshare.
  2. Working plan: likely method, session count, and what you will practise between visits.
  3. Ongoing work: skills, exposure where needed, and review of what actually changed.
  4. Close or maintenance: longer gaps once you can run the tools yourself.
