How does a child assessment session work?

A first child assessment is a structured visit of about 45 to 60 minutes. Parents describe history. The child is observed through play or tasks. You leave with a recommended plan, likely session count, and whether formal testing is needed next.

In-clinic at Omar Hospital is the better default for first child visits. Play and testing need the room. Online follow-ups work later for parent coaching. Saturday clinic hours are 2:00 to 5:00 PM. Fee is PKR 5,000 in clinic, PKR 4,000 online.

What happens, step by step

  1. Intake with the parent: pregnancy, milestones, school, sleep, previous reports.
  2. Time with the child: play, conversation, or tasks matched to age.
  3. Early formulation: what is likely, what is still unclear, what can wait.
  4. Plan: therapy, testing, school liaison, parent sessions, or a mix.

Young children (2 to 5) spend more of the hour in play and parent guidance. Older children can sit with a task. Teens often need part of the hour without the parent in the chair. Ms. Tehreem will say which format fits. You do not have to guess.

After the first visit

Some families need two or three assessment hours before a clear picture. That is normal for autism and learning disability questions. Starting treatment on a guess wastes more money than an extra assessment hour.

What is the parent's role in child therapy?

Parents are part of the treatment, not the audience. Younger children need you in the process for assessment and home practice. Teens need some private sessions plus separate parent guidance. Home practice is where most of the change actually happens.

Clinics that treat the child as a solo patient and the parent as a taxi service get weak results. A behaviour plan lives at dinner, homework time, and the school gate. If only the child "does therapy," the week wins.

Younger children

You will be in the room a lot. You will also get coaching: how to respond to a tantrum without feeding it, how to practise a speech target for 8 minutes a day, how to stop two adults in the house running two different rule books. Inconsistent homes undo good sessions.

Older children and teens

Some hours are one to one so the young person can speak freely. You still get a parent session. You will hear themes and skills, not a transcript. That boundary is the point. Break it and the teen stops talking.
