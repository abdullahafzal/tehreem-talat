About Ms. Tehreem Talat

A Trusted Clinical Psychologist in Lahore

Ms. Tehreem Talat is a Consultant Clinical Psychologist with over 14 years of experience across some of Pakistan's most respected medical and educational institutions, including Jinnah Hospital, Fountain House, Punjab Institute of Mental Health (PIMH), and the Beaconhouse School System.

She works with both adults and children, offering evidence-based therapy including Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT), individual psychotherapy, psychological testing, and behavioural interventions. Her approach is warm, structured, and deeply personalised.

Beyond clinical practice, Ms. Tehreem has contributed to the field as a visiting lecturer of Cognitive Psychology at Government College University, Lahore, bringing an academic depth that informs every aspect of her therapeutic work.

Child Psychology
Ms. Tehreem works with children from toddler age through to late adolescence (18+). For very young children (2 to 5 years), sessions typically involve more parent guidance and play-based observation. For older children and teens, structured therapy and assessment tools are used. There is no minimum age. Early intervention almost always leads to better long-term outcomes.
Common signs that a child may benefit from psychological assessment include: significant speech or language delays, difficulties with social interaction or making eye contact, extreme or persistent behavioural challenges, sudden changes in mood or behaviour, struggling significantly at school despite good effort, or any concern a parent has about their child's emotional or cognitive development. Early assessment is always worthwhile.
For younger children, parents are typically involved throughout the process, both during assessment and as part of the intervention plan. For older children and teens, some sessions may be one-to-one to allow the child to speak freely, with separate parent guidance sessions alongside. Ms. Tehreem will advise on the best format based on your child's age and needs.
